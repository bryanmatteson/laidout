# Code of conduct

Laidout's community is committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment
for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experiences and communication styles.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and repair harm.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Send conduct concerns to the contact address published on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bryanmatteson) with `Laidout
conduct report` in the subject. Enforcement actions include editing or removing
contributions and temporarily or permanently restricting participation.
Reports remain confidential to the extent required for investigation and
participant safety.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
